01 · Brand research

youthaudiolifestyle

boAt is an Indian consumer audio and wearables brand, started in 2016 by Aman Gupta and Sameer Mehta under Imagine Marketing. It grew online, selling earphones, headphones, speakers and smartwatches direct to young buyers at accessible prices. Its fans call themselves boAtheads. The brand sells less a gadget and more a way of showing who you are.

Brand identity

Youth · audio · lifestyle. Products that are fashionable, affordable and durable: technology worn as part of an outfit, not kept in a drawer.

Keywords

Identity · experience · emotion · connection · individuality

Who it speaks to

Young and wild explorers. A tribe of go-getters: young rebels, optimists, trendsetters and free spirits who think out of the box.

What it stands for

Music as everyday self-expression, made accessible to everyone, with confidence and a sense of fun.

Its audience

boAt talks mainly to Indians aged about 18 to 35: Gen Z and young millennials, from college students to people in their first jobs, in big metros and increasingly in smaller cities too. They are digital first, discover and buy online, and want a product that looks good, works well and stays within budget. Music is part of how they commute, train, game, study and hang out with friends, and what they wear on their ears says something about who they are. They treat boAt less as a gadget brand and more as a tribe they belong to.

02 · Concept

The Trail

A workspace that thinks, behaves and feels like boAt.

Concept statement

Sound is never static. It travels, connects, changes moods and brings people together. The workspace translates this energy into fluid spaces that inspire focus, spark collaboration and celebrate individuality.

The task

Understand the brand beyond its logo and products. Understand its philosophy and personality. Then design a space that thinks, behaves and feels like boAt.

The space it needs

A workplace catering to diverse work styles. Each part has its own identity but belongs to a whole system. Bold, expressive, positive and energetic. Exploration, connection and rhythm, with functionality and fun. Built on the relationship between sound, energy, people and movement.

04 · Layout

The rendered floor plan

One continuous trail organises the whole floor. It starts at the main entrance, wraps around the reception and branding wall, and carries people past every kind of work setting before it reaches the quiet rooms on the glazed edge. Nothing is a corridor; every route is also a place to pause, meet or work.

Click the plan to zoom in on any part of it.

Why this plan works for boAt India

The trail is the hero

A planted pebble trail threads through the whole floor, from reception to the far alcoves. It replaces the corridor with a garden walk, so the most memorable part of the office is the one everyone uses every day, and greenery is never more than a few steps from any desk.

Circulation that builds curiosity

No route runs straight. Passages swell and narrow, from 5 ft along the entrance to almost 11 ft at the heart of the working zone, and every bend holds back the next space until you reach it. Moving through the office becomes a small discovery, not a commute.

A new view at every turn

Each turn frames something different: the branding wall as you arrive, the tree and collab tables on one side, the alcoves and sit-outs on the other, the working zone glimpsed through planting. People see colleagues, greenery and daylight in turn, never a blank wall.

Loud at the door, quiet at the glass

Visitors, waiting, the pantry and semi-public collab stay close to the entrance. Teams work in the centre. The boardroom, leadership cabin, meeting rooms and phone booths take the quiet glazed edge, with executive lounges as a soft buffer. Planting, levels and carpet make the shift, not walls.

Many ways to work on one floor

Formal desks, a touchdown bench, informal tables, phone booths, alcoves, sit-outs and a listening lab. A young, fast-moving team picks the setting that suits the task: heads-down work, a quick huddle, a client call or a product test.

It behaves like boAt

Expressive but accessible, social but personal. The plan gives the brand's balance of individual and collective a physical form, and does it with standard fit-out materials inside the existing columns, shafts, washrooms and glazing, so it is realistic to build and easy to run.
